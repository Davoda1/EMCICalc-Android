# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial release of EMCICalc Android.

- Exact and approximate calculation, symbolic mathematics, units and currency.
- Expression, Classic and RPN workflows, programmer tools and custom keypads.
- Graph, matrix, statistics and Finance workspaces; bounded Steps explanations.
- Three keypad layouts, eight themes, nine languages and adaptive orientation.
- Touch help, accessibility labels, saved history, registers and workspace exchange.
- ARM64 build recipes for SDK 36 and native 16 KiB alignment.
- Automated source/host checks and manual source archives with checksums.

Build instructions and device validation requirements are linked from the README.
